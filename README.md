# Turn-based strategy game
 
<pre>1 - Introduction 
The project assignment subject is inspired by the novel The Lord of the Rings,
by British author J. R. R. Tolkien, and consists on the development of a simple 
turn-based strategy game. In this project, you will develop a turn-based
strategy game set in the sprawling landscapes of Middle-Earth. From the Shire
to Mordor, players will build their strongholds, gather resources, and command
their armies in a quest for dominion. Will you stand with the forces of good
in Rivendell and Gondor, or will you succumb to the dark powers of Sauron in
Mordor? The fate of Middle-Earth is in your hands...</pre>
